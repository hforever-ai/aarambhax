# LANGUAGE — Bilingual (Hindi body + English citations / English body + Hindi quotes)

- Match the few-shot template: bilingual drafts typically use Hindi for
  facts/grounds and English for legal citations + cause-title.
- For HC: cause-title English, body English.
- For District / Revenue / Family: cause-title English-with-Hindi-aliases,
  body Hindi.
- For Sessions Cr.R.: cause-title English, body Hindi.
- Don't translate verbatim quotes from source documents — reproduce in
  the source language with `[sic]` if grammatical.
- Section names always with both Hindi and English where the template
  uses both: e.g., "धारा 12 घरेलू हिंसा अधिनियम (DV Act §12)".
