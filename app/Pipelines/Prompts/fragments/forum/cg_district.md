# FORUM — Chhattisgarh District / Sessions / Family / Magistrate Courts

## Common districts: Jashpur, Raigarh, Janjgir-Champa, Pathalgaon, Bilaspur, Raipur, Durg, Korba

## Format requirements

- **Default Language:** If the case state language is `hi`, use Devanagari throughout the body. Cause title and court names may keep English if bilingual presentation is expected or for specific types of English-only filings. Otherwise, generally Hindi for district courts.

- **Heading style (Hindi):**
  ```
  न्यायालय श्रीमान न्यायिक दण्डाधिकारी प्रथम श्रेणी, [तहसील], जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)
  ```
  (or प्रधान न्यायाधीश, परिवार न्यायालय / अतिरिक्त सत्र न्यायाधीश / जिला न्यायाधीश / विशेष न्यायाधीश, पोक्सो एक्ट / मोटर दुर्घटना दावा न्यायाधिकरण आदि, as applicable)

- **Heading style (English):**
  ```
  IN THE COURT OF [Judicial Magistrate First Class / Sessions Judge / etc.],
  [District] (CG)
  ```

- **Case-type Prefix:**
  - **Civil:** वाद पत्र (Civil Suit), एम.जे.सी. (Misc. Judicial Case), निष्पादन प्रकरण (Execution Case), भरण-पोषण आवेदन (Maintenance Application), विवाह विच्छेद याचिका (Divorce Petition), मोटर दुर्घटना दावा प्रकरण (MACT).
  - **Criminal:** परिवाद पत्र (Complaint Case), फौजदारी प्रकरण (Criminal Case), एन.आई. एक्ट प्रकरण (NI Act Case), जमानत आवेदन (Bail Application), प्रोटेक्शन आर्डर आवेदन (Protection Order Application - DV Act).
  - **Other:** नामान्तरण आवेदन (Mutation Application), भूमि सुधार प्रकरण (Land Reform Case).

- **Case number:** प्रकरण क्रमांक / Case No. ____ / [year]
  (e.g., `सिविल सूट क्रमांक ____ / [वर्ष]`, `परिवाद पत्र क्रमांक ____ / [वर्ष]`)

- **Cause-title structure:**
  - Full names + parentage + age + residence (full address with Police Station and District). Petitioner/Applicant/Plaintiff first, then VERSUS, then Respondents/Defendants.
  - **Individual:** `[नाम] पिता/पति [पिता/पति का नाम], आयु लगभग [आयु] वर्ष, निवासी [पूर्ण पता, थाना], जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)`
  - **Government/Statutory Body Respondents:** Addressed through the head of the local office. E.g., "कलेक्टर, जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)" OR "पुलिस अधीक्षक, जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)" OR "तहसीलदार, तहसील [तहसील], जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)".
  - **Other Entities:** `[संस्था का नाम], प्रोपराइटर/मैनेजर [व्यक्ति का नाम], पता [पूर्ण पता], जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)`

- **Parties labelled:** Per Hindi register based on the proceeding type:
  `आवेदक/आवेदिका` (Applicant), `अनावेदक/अनावेदिका` (Respondent), `परिवादी` (Complainant), `अभियुक्त` (Accused), `वादी` (Plaintiff), `प्रतिवादी` (Defendant).

- **Particulars sections:** Essential particulars (name, age, address of parties) must be clearly stated within the Cause Title or the initial paragraphs. "Subject Matter in Brief" is highly recommended.

- **Numbered facts paragraphs:** Use Hindi numerals (१. २. ३.) or Arabic (1. 2. 3.) to detail facts chronologically.

- **Use formal Hindi legal register:** Examples: "व्यथित महिला" (aggrieved woman), "साझी गृहस्थी" (shared household), "घरेलू नातेदारी" (domestic relationship), "नामांतरण" (mutation), "खसरा क्रमांक" (survey number), "विक्रय विलेख" (sale deed), "श्रीमान न्यायाधीश महोदय" (Hon'ble Judge Sir).

- **Document-list:** A list of documents (दस्तावेज १, दस्तावेज २, …) appended at the end, with each fact paragraph referring to the relevant document number.

- **Standard prayer (निवेदन):** "अतः न्यायालय से सादर निवेदन है कि..." followed by specific reliefs.

- **Affidavit:** Mandatory for most applications, petitions, and plaints. Must be on a separate page with paragraph-wise verification, signed by the deponent, and attested by an Oath Commissioner or Notary Public.

- **Citation priority:** Supreme Court > High Court > Local District Court judgments.

## Counsel's standard block

```
स्थान: [जिला/तहसील]                       [हस्ताक्षर]
दिनांक:  ___ / ___ / ____                 [अधिवक्ता का नाम]
                                          आवेदक/वादी/परिवादी के अधिवक्ता
                                          बार काउंसिल पंजीयन क्रमांक: CG/____/____
```

## Drafter Section Order

The following sections must be produced by the drafter in the specified order:

1. **Court Heading (न्यायालय का शीर्षक)**
   - **Heading Text:** `न्यायालय श्रीमान [पदनाम], [तहसील], जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)` OR `IN THE COURT OF [Designation], [Tehsil/District], [District] (CG)`
   - **Content:** The exact name and location of the court where the filing is being made.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

2. **Case Type & Number (प्रकरण प्रकार एवं क्रमांक)**
   - **Heading Text:** `प्रकरण क्रमांक / Case No. ____ / [year]`
   - **Content:** Specify the case type (e.g., `सिविल सूट / Civil Suit`, `परिवाद पत्र / Complaint Case`, `भरण-पोषण आवेदन / Maintenance Application`) followed by the placeholder for the case number and year.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes (number filled by court registry)

3. **Cause Title (मामले का शीर्षक)**
   - **Content:**
     - `[नाम] पिता/पति [नाम], आयु लगभग [आयु] वर्ष, निवासी [पूर्ण पता, थाना], जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)` — followed by `(वादी/Plaintiff)` / `(आवेदक/Applicant)` / `(परिवादी/Complainant)`
     - `VERSUS / बनाम`
     - `[नाम] पिता/पति [नाम], आयु लगभग [आयु] वर्ष, निवासी [पूर्ण पता, थाना], जिला [जिला] (छ.ग.)` — followed by `(प्रतिवादी/Defendant)` / `(अनावेदक/Respondent)` / `(अभियुक्त/Accused)`
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

4. **Subject Matter in Brief (विषय वस्तु का संक्षिप्त विवरण)**
   - **Heading Text:** `विषय वस्तु का संक्षिप्त विवरण / Subject Matter in Brief`
   - **Content:** A single, concise sentence or short paragraph describing the overall nature and purpose of the petition/plaint/application.
   - **Mandatory:** Highly Recommended (often integrated into the first body paragraph)

5. **Petition / Plaint / Application Body (याचिका / वाद पत्र / आवेदन पत्र)**
   - **Heading Text:** `याचिका / वाद पत्र / आवेदन पत्र` (or specific name: `परिवाद पत्र`, `जमानत आवेदन`)
   - **Content:**
     - Numbered paragraphs (१., २., ३., etc.) presenting the facts chronologically.
     - State all relevant details, dates, incidents, and legal points clearly.
     - Each fact paragraph references supporting documents (e.g., "दस्तावेज क्रमांक १ अनुसार...").
     - Conclude with a statement reaffirming the jurisdiction of the court.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

6. **List of Documents (दस्तावेज सूची)**
   - **Heading Text:** `दस्तावेज सूची / List of Documents`
   - **Content:** A sequentially numbered list of all documents being filed, with a brief description of each.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

7. **Prayer (प्रार्थना / निवेदन)**
   - **Heading Text:** `प्रार्थना / निवेदन / Prayer`
   - **Content:** Begins with `अतः न्यायालय से सादर निवेदन है कि...` — clearly list each specific relief — close with `...एवं अन्य कोई भी राहत जो न्यायालय उचित समझे प्रदान करें।`
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

8. **Counsel Signature Block (अधिवक्ता का हस्ताक्षर ब्लॉक)**
   - **Content:** Use the standard block above (स्थान, दिनांक, हस्ताक्षर, नाम, बार काउंसिल क्रमांक).
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

9. **Verification / Affidavit (सत्यापन / शपथ पत्र)**
   - **Heading Text:** `सत्यापन / शपथ पत्र / Verification / Affidavit`
   - **Content:** Must be on a separate page. Deponent's statement, paragraph-wise verification, signed by deponent and attested by Oath Commissioner or Notary Public.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes (for most original filings)
