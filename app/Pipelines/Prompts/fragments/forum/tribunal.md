# FORUM — Tribunals

Covers: CG RERA + Appellate Tribunal, MACT (Motor Accidents Claims), CG Consumer Commission, Labour Court / Industrial Tribunal, ITAT, CESTAT, GST Appellate, NCLT (jurisdictional bench Cuttack/Kolkata for CG), DRT, Cooperative Tribunal.

## Default Language

- **English** is the default for most tribunals (CG RERA + Appellate Tribunal, ITAT, CESTAT, GST Appellate Tribunal, NCLT, DRT, Cooperative Tribunal).
- **Hindi (Devanagari script)** is commonly used and often preferred for Consumer Commissions and MACT cases, especially where parties are Hindi-speaking or local rules permit/encourage Hindi filings. Filings can be bilingual or predominantly in either language as per tribunal practice.

## Format requirements

- **Header:** `BEFORE THE [NAME OF TRIBUNAL] AT [LOCATION]`
  - Examples:
    - `BEFORE THE CHHATTISGARH REAL ESTATE REGULATORY AUTHORITY AT RAIPUR`
    - `MOTOR ACCIDENTS CLAIMS TRIBUNAL, BILASPUR (C.G.)`

- **Case-type and number prefix:** Use the tribunal-specific case-type prefix, type, and year. Number part is typically left blank (`____`) or marked "To be assigned" initially.
  - Examples: `Complaint No. M-____ / [YEAR]` (RERA), `Claim Petition No. ____ / [YEAR]` (MACT), `Consumer Complaint No. ____ / [YEAR]` (Consumer Commission).

- **Cause-title structure:** Full name(s), father's/husband's name, age, complete residential address with PIN, district, contact number, email. For corporate/government entities: full legal name and official address.
  - Petitioner/Applicant/Complainant details first, then VERSUS, then Respondent(s) details.

- **Govt/Corporate respondents:** Addressed as "through its [Designation, e.g., Secretary, Managing Director, Authorized Officer], [Department/Company Name], [Full Address, City, State, PIN Code]".

- **Specific statutory section invoked:** Clearly state the relevant section(s) of the Act under which the petition is filed.
  - Examples: `COMPLAINT UNDER SECTION 31 OF THE REAL ESTATE (REGULATION AND DEVELOPMENT) ACT, 2016` / `CLAIM PETITION UNDER SECTION 166 OF THE MOTOR VEHICLES ACT, 1988`.

- **Synopsis & Brief Facts:** Concise summary of the case and the primary relief sought.

- **Numbered facts paragraphs:** All factual averments in distinct, serially numbered paragraphs.

- **Pleadings / Grounds for Relief:** Clear, concise paragraphs stating the legal and factual basis for the relief sought (lettered or numbered).

- **Sworn affidavit:** Mandatory on a separate page, with paragraph-wise verification.

- **Citation priority:** SCC > AIR > CGLJ > Other Law Reports.

- **Tribunal-specific evidence rules:** All documents relied upon listed in a separate section and annexed with clear numbering (e.g., Annexure A-1, A-2).

## Drafter Section Order

The following sections must be produced by the drafter in the specified order:

| # | Exact Heading Text | Content | Mandatory? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **BEFORE THE [NAME OF TRIBUNAL] AT [LOCATION]** | The complete official name and location of the tribunal. | Mandatory |
| 2 | **[Case-type prefix] No. ____ / [YEAR]** | Tribunal-specific case-type prefix (e.g., `Complaint No. M-____ / 2024`, `Claim Petition No. ____ / 2024`). Registry assigns the number. | Mandatory |
| 3 | **[PARTY NAMES AND DETAILS — CAUSE TITLE]** | Complainant/Applicant/Petitioner full details → VERSUS → Respondent(s) full details (including "through its [Designation]..." for corporates/govt). | Mandatory |
| 4 | **PETITION UNDER SECTION [X] OF THE [ACT NAME], [YEAR]** | The specific statutory provision(s) under which the petition is filed + full name of the governing Act with year. | Mandatory |
| 5 | **SYNOPSIS / BRIEF FACTS OF THE CASE** | Concise summary — key events, nature of dispute, and primary relief sought. | Mandatory |
| 6 | **LIST OF DATES** | Chronological listing of all significant dates and events from genesis of dispute to date of filing. | Optional |
| 7 | **PARTICULARS OF THE PETITIONER / APPLICANT / COMPLAINANT** | Detailed personal information and background of the filing party. | Mandatory |
| 8 | **PARTICULARS OF THE RESPONDENT(S)** | Detailed information about the opposing party/parties. | Mandatory |
| 9 | **PARTICULARS OF THE ORDER AGAINST WHICH THIS PETITION IS MADE** | Order number, date, issuing authority, brief description. Only for appeals/revisions challenging a specific order. | Mandatory (if applicable) |
| 10 | **JURISDICTION** | Clear statement affirming the tribunal has territorial and pecuniary jurisdiction. | Mandatory |
| 11 | **FACTS OF THE CASE** | Detailed, chronological narrative of all relevant factual averments in serially numbered paragraphs. Avoid legal arguments here. | Mandatory |
| 12 | **GROUNDS FOR RELIEF / PLEADINGS / ISSUES FOR DETERMINATION** | Legal and factual arguments forming the basis for the relief sought. Lettered (A, B, C...) or serially numbered paragraphs. | Mandatory |
| 13 | **RELIEF SOUGHT / PRAYER** | Precise and specific remedies, orders, or directions requested from the tribunal. Each relief as a distinct point. | Mandatory |
| 14 | **VALUATION** (if applicable) | Monetary value of the claim for pecuniary jurisdiction and court fees assessment. | Mandatory (for tribunals requiring pecuniary valuation) |
| 15 | **COURT FEES** | Declaration confirming the prescribed tribunal fee has been paid as per relevant rules. | Mandatory |
| 16 | **AFFIDAVIT** | Declaration that a sworn affidavit with paragraph-wise verification is annexed and forms part of the petition. | Mandatory |
| 17 | **LIST OF DOCUMENTS (ANNEXURES)** | Comprehensive serially numbered list of all documentary evidence and annexures (e.g., Annexure A-1, P-1). | Mandatory |
| — | **Place: [City of Tribunal] / Date: ___ / ___ / ____** | Place and date of signing. | Mandatory |
| — | **[COUNSEL SIGNATURE BLOCK]** | Counsel's signature, name, designation, enrollment number, phone, and email per the standard block above. | Mandatory |
