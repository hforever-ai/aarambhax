# FORUM — Chhattisgarh Family Court

Common matters: HMA divorce/restitution/judicial separation, §125 BNSS / §144 BNSS maintenance, DV Act §12, custody, adoption, Special Marriage Act, Muslim Personal Law applications.

## Format requirements (Chhattisgarh Family Court Rules & Practice)

- **Header:**
  ```
  न्यायालय श्रीमान प्रधान न्यायाधीश महोदय, परिवार न्यायालय, [जिला] (छत्तीसगढ़)
  ```

- **Case-type prefix:**
  - हिन्दू विवाह अधिनियम के अंतर्गत (Under HMA)
  - विशेष विवाह अधिनियम के अंतर्गत (Under SMA)
  - घरेलू हिंसा से महिला संरक्षण अधिनियम के अंतर्गत (Under DV Act)
  - दंड प्रक्रिया संहिता की धारा 125/144 के अंतर्गत (Under CrPC/BNSS §125/144)
  - दत्तक ग्रहण आवेदन (Adoption Application)
  - अभिभावक एवं प्रतिपाल्य अधिनियम के अंतर्गत (Under Guardians and Wards Act)
  - मुस्लिम व्यक्तिगत विधि के अंतर्गत (Under Muslim Personal Law)
  - Numbering: `[प्रकार] प्रकरण क्रमांक: ............ / [वर्ष]`

- **Cause-title structure:**
  - **आवेदक/आवेदिका (Applicant/Petitioner):** Full Name, Father's/Husband's Name, Age, Profession, Residence (Full postal address with PIN code), District.
  - **VERSUS / बनाम**
  - **अनावेदक/अनावेदिका (Respondent):** Full Name, Father's/Husband's Name, Age, Profession, Residence (Full postal address with PIN code), District.
  - For DV Act matters use: **व्यथित महिला (Aggrieved Woman)** as Applicant, and **प्रत्यर्थी (Respondent)** as Respondent.

- **Default language:** Hindi Devanagari script is strongly preferred for all pleadings. English may be used for specific technical terms or citations.

- **Facts paragraphs:** Numbered paragraphs for factual narration.

- **Grounds paragraphs:** Lettered paragraphs (अ, ब, स...) for legal grounds.

- **Attached annexures:** marriage certificate, Aadhaar, school records of children, prior complaints, photographs of injuries, bank statements, income proof.

- **For maintenance:** income evidence of respondent + financial need of applicant must be specifically pleaded and supported by documents.

- **Affidavit:** Mandatory, on separate page, paragraph-wise verification.

- **Citation priority:** SCC > AIR > CGLJ > Law Reports.

- **Counsel signature block:** At bottom right.

## Mandatory Clauses

- **No Other Proceedings Pending:** Explicitly state no similar proceedings are pending elsewhere.
- **No Collusion (कोई मिलीभगत नहीं है):** Essential for divorce and judicial separation petitions.
- **Valuation & Court Fee Statement:** Confirmation of proper valuation and payment of court fees.
- **Jurisdiction Statement:** Clearly establish the court's jurisdiction.

## Counsel's standard block

```
स्थान: [जिला]                                     [हस्ताक्षर]
दिनांक:  ___ / ___ / ____                          [अधिवक्ता का नाम]

                                                  बार कौंसिल पंजीकरण क्रमांक: CG/____/____
```

## Drafter Section Order (परिवार न्यायालय हेतु प्रारूपण क्रम)

The following sections must be produced by the drafter in the specified order:

1. **Court Heading (न्यायालय का शीर्षक)**
   - **Heading Text:** `न्यायालय श्रीमान प्रधान न्यायाधीश महोदय, परिवार न्यायालय, [जिला] (छत्तीसगढ़)`
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

2. **Case Type & Number (प्रकरण का प्रकार एवं क्रमांक)**
   - **Heading Text:** `[प्रकार] प्रकरण क्रमांक: ............ / [वर्ष]`
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

3. **Cause Title (वाद-शीर्षक)**
   - **Content:** आवेदक/आवेदिका full particulars → VERSUS / बनाम → अनावेदक/अनावेदिका full particulars. For DV Act: व्यथित महिला / प्रत्यर्थीगण.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

4. **Particulars of the Applicant (आवेदक/आवेदिका का विवरण)**
   - **Content:** Full name, parentage, age, religion, profession, complete residential address (with PIN), contact details.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

5. **Particulars of the Respondent(s) (अनावेदक/अनावेदिका का विवरण)**
   - **Content:** Full name, parentage, age, religion, profession, complete residential address (with PIN), relationship with applicant.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

6. **Subject Matter in Brief (आवेदन का विषय)**
   - **Content:** Single concise sentence stating the purpose of the petition. E.g., "यह आवेदन हिन्दू विवाह अधिनियम की धारा 13 के अंतर्गत विवाह विच्छेद की डिक्री हेतु प्रस्तुत किया गया है।"
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

7. **Synopsis / Brief Facts (प्रकरण का संक्षिप्त विवरण)**
   - **Content:** Brief overview — core issue and critical dates (marriage date, separation date, main grievance).
   - **Mandatory:** Recommended

8. **List of Dates (तिथियों की सूची)**
   - **Content:** Chronological list of significant dates and corresponding events.
   - **Mandatory:** Optional (Highly recommended for complex cases)

9. **Facts (तथ्य)**
   - **Content:** Numbered paragraphs in chronological order — establishment of marriage/relationship, details of children, events leading to dispute, specific incidents. For maintenance: income evidence and financial need must be specifically pleaded.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

10. **Grounds (आधार)**
    - **Content:** Lettered paragraphs (अ, ब, स...) stating legal grounds linked to the facts.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

11. **Jurisdiction (क्षेत्राधिकार)**
    - **Content:** Explicit statement that the Family Court at [District] has territorial and pecuniary jurisdiction (parties resided last together / marriage solemnized / cause of action arose).
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

12. **Valuation & Court Fee (मूल्यांकन एवं न्यायालय शुल्क)**
    - **Content:** Valuation of the subject matter for court fee purposes + confirmation that court fee has been paid.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

13. **No Other Proceedings Pending (कोई अन्य कार्यवाही लंबित नहीं है)**
    - **Content:** Declaration that no other similar proceedings between the parties on the same subject matter are pending in any other court.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

14. **No Collusion (कोई मिलीभगत नहीं है)**
    - **Content:** Mandatory declaration in divorce or judicial separation petitions that there is no collusion between the parties.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes (for HMA/SMA divorce/judicial separation)

15. **Prayer (प्रार्थना)**
    - **Content:** Clear and specific relief(s) sought. E.g., "अतः माननीय न्यायालय से प्रार्थना है कि आवेदक के पक्ष में अनावेदक के विरुद्ध विवाह विच्छेद की डिक्री पारित की जावे..."
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

16. **List of Documents (दस्तावेजों की सूची)**
    - **Content:** List of all annexures and supporting documents attached with the petition.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

17. **Counsel Signature Block**
    - **Content:** Use the standard block above.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

18. **Affidavit (शपथ पत्र)**
    - **Content:** Separate page — affidavit by the applicant(s) verifying each paragraph of the petition, sworn before an Oath Commissioner/Notary Public.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes
