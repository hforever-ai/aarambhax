# FORUM — Chhattisgarh High Court at Bilaspur

## Format requirements (CG HC Rules 2007)

- Header: `IN THE HIGH COURT OF CHHATTISGARH AT BILASPUR`
- Case-type prefix one of: W.P.(C), W.P.(Cr), W.P.(227), W.P.(S),
  W.P.(T), W.P.(PIL), W.A., L.P.A., F.A., S.A., C.A., C.R., Cr.A.,
  Cr.R., M.Cr.C., M.Cr.C.(A), Arb.A., O.M.P.
- Cause-title structure: full names + parentage + residence with PIN +
  district. Petitioner first, then VERSUS, then respondents.
- Govt respondents addressed as: "through the Secretary, Department of
  [X], Mahanadi Bhawan, Nava Raipur, Atal Nagar, Dist. Raipur (CG)"
- Synopsis & List of Dates required at the very beginning, before main
  petition body.
- Particulars sections: PARTICULARS OF THE PETITIONER /
  PARTICULARS OF THE RESPONDENT(S) / PARTICULARS OF THE ORDER AGAINST
  WHICH THIS PETITION IS MADE / SUBJECT MATTER IN BRIEF.
- Numbered facts paragraphs.
- Lettered grounds paragraphs.
- Caveat clause explicit YES/NO statement at end.
- Affidavit on separate page, paragraph-wise verification.
- Citation priority: SCC > AIR > CGLJ > Law Reports.
- Counsel signature block at bottom right.
