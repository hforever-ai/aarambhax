# FORUM — Supreme Court of India

Common: SLP (Civil), SLP (Criminal), Curative Petition, Review, Reply to Contempt Notice, Civil Appeal, Criminal Appeal.

## Format requirements (Supreme Court Rules 2013)

- **Default Language:** English only.

- **Header Structure:**
  ```
  IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
  [CIVIL / CRIMINAL / APPELLATE / ORIGINAL / WRIT / REVIEW / CURATIVE] JURISDICTION
  SPECIAL LEAVE PETITION ([CIVIL/CRIMINAL]) / CIVIL APPEAL / CRIMINAL APPEAL / WRIT PETITION (CIVIL) / ETC.
  NO. _______ OF [YEAR]
  ```

- **Cause-Title Structure (Memo of Parties):**
  - Full names, parentage (father's/husband's name), age, occupation, complete residential address (with PIN code), email address, and phone number for all parties.
  - Petitioner/Appellant first, then VERSUS, then Respondents.
  - **Government Respondents:** "The Union of India, through the Secretary, Ministry of [X], Government of India, New Delhi" OR "The State of [X], through its Chief Secretary/Secretary, Department of [Y], [City, State]".

- **Mandatory Preliminary Sections:**
  - **Index:** Compulsory.
  - **Memo of Parties:** Compulsory, detailed as above.
  - **Synopsis:** Compulsory, a concise statement of facts and points of law.
  - **List of Dates/Chronology of Events:** Compulsory, chronological summary of relevant events.

- **Content Formatting:**
  - Numbered paragraphs for facts.
  - Lettered paragraphs for grounds of challenge/questions of law.

- **AOR Mandatory:** All petitions/appeals in the Supreme Court must be filed through an Advocate-on-Record (AOR). AOR's signature and registration number are mandatory.

- **Caveat:** If a caveat has been lodged by a respondent, state so and serve a copy to the caveator. If none lodged, include a declaration to that effect.

- **Affidavit:** Compulsory. Separate page, paragraph-wise verification, attested by Oath Commissioner or Notary Public.

- **Citation Priority:** Strictly SCC > AIR > SC Online (SCCOnLine) > other Law Reports/Journals.

## Drafter Section Order

The following sections must be produced by the drafter in the specified order:

1. **Cover Page**
   - **Content:** Case type, number (if allotted), parties' names (brief), counsel's name and AOR number.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

2. **Index (List of Contents)**
   - **Content:** Comprehensive list of all sections, documents, and annexures with page numbers.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

3. **Memo of Parties (List of Parties)**
   - **Content:** Full names, parentage, age, occupation, complete residential address (with PIN), email, and phone of all Petitioners/Appellants and Respondents.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

4. **Synopsis**
   - **Content:** Concise summary of the case, the impugned order, the questions of law arising, and the grounds on which relief is sought. Not exceeding two pages.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

5. **List of Dates / Chronology of Events**
   - **Content:** Chronological summary of all material dates and events, including dates of orders, appeals, etc.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

6. **List of Cases / Authorities** (if numerous)
   - **Content:** List of judgments, statutes, and other legal authorities to be relied upon.
   - **Mandatory:** Optional (good practice if numerous)

7. **Application for Exemption** (if seeking exemption from filing certified copy, official translation, etc.)
   - **Content:** Specific application with reasons for the exemption sought.
   - **Mandatory:** Yes (if seeking exemption)

8. **Main Petition Header**
   - **Content:** The header as specified above (Jurisdiction, Petition Type, No., Year).
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

9. **Salutation to the Court**
   - **Content:** `TO THE HON'BLE THE CHIEF JUSTICE OF INDIA AND HIS COMPANION JUDGES OF THE HON'BLE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA.`
   - **Mandatory:** Yes

10. **Interlocutory Application (IA) / Prayer for Interim Relief** (if separate)
    - **Content:** Specific application for interim orders (stay of proceedings, stay of impugned order) with supporting grounds.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes (if seeking interim relief)

11. **Cause Title (Petitioner/Appellant vs. Respondent)**
    - **Content:** Brief names of the Petitioner/Appellant and Respondent(s) as they appear in the Memo of Parties.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

12. **Introduction / Preamble to the Petition**
    - **Content:** `MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:` or similar opening, stating the nature of the petition.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

13. **Statement of Facts**
    - **Content:** Clear, concise, and chronological narration of all material facts in numbered paragraphs.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

14. **Questions of Law** (especially for SLPs and Appeals)
    - **Content:** Precise formulation of substantial questions of law for consideration by the Supreme Court.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes for SLPs (Order XXI, Rule 3(1)(g))

15. **Grounds for Special Leave / Appeal / Petition**
    - **Content:** Detailed legal grounds and arguments challenging the impugned order/judgment, in lettered paragraphs. Legal reasoning and precedents primarily discussed here.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

16. **Interim Prayer** (if integrated into petition body)
    - **Content:** Specific prayer for interim relief if not made through a separate IA.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes (if interim relief is sought)

17. **Final Prayer**
    - **Content:** Main relief(s) sought from the Supreme Court (e.g., "Set aside the impugned order," "Grant Special Leave to Appeal").
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

18. **Declaration / Undertaking**
    - **Content:** Standard declarations — "That no other Special Leave Petition/appeal has been filed by the petitioner/appellant against the impugned judgment/order"; declaration regarding caveat; any undertaking required by rules/practice.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes (Order XXI Rule 3(1)(k) for SLPs)

19. **Counsel Signature Block**
    - **Content:** Use the AOR standard block above.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

20. **Verification (Affidavit)**
    - **Content:** Separate document — paragraph-wise verification stating what is true to knowledge and what to belief. Duly sworn and attested.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes

21. **List of Documents / Annexures**
    - **Content:** List of all documents annexed to the petition, arranged chronologically or by relevance.
    - **Mandatory:** Yes
