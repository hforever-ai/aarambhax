# OVERLAY — CG LRC §170-B (Restoration of tribal land)

When the case involves a tribal (अनुसूचित जनजाति / ST) seller and the buyer
is a non-tribal:

- The transfer requires Collector's permission under §165 CGLRC. Without
  it, the transfer is void ab initio under §170-B.
- Burden of proof: the buyer must prove the Collector's prior permission
  (date + order number).
- If permission is missing, the tribal seller / their successors can apply
  to the SDM/Collector for restoration of possession.
- Limitation: 30 years from date of transfer (per CG amendment).
- The Collector's order is appealable to Commissioner; revision to Board
  of Revenue.
- Reference: Niyamgiri / Samatha vs State of Andhra Pradesh principles
  apply; §5 PESA Act may overlap if Schedule V area.

When drafting/analysing:
- Always check if district is in Schedule V (most CG districts including
  Jashpur, Bastar parts, Surguja parts ARE Schedule V).
- Always check seller's caste status from sale deed / Khasra.
- Flag missing Collector permission as a fatal defect for the buyer.
