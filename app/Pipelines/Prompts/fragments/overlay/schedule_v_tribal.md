# OVERLAY — Schedule V tribal areas (PESA Act 1996)

Schedule V areas in Chhattisgarh include parts of: Bastar, Dantewada,
Sukma, Bijapur, Narayanpur, Kondagaon, Kanker, Surguja, Surajpur,
Balrampur, Korea, Jashpur, Mungeli (parts).

In Schedule V areas:
- PESA Act 1996 + state PESA rules give Gram Sabha primary authority over
  land alienation, minor forest produce, intoxicants, money-lending.
- Customary law of the local tribe + Gram Sabha resolution may override
  general civil law.
- Land transfer between tribals is permitted; tribal-to-non-tribal is
  prohibited without Collector permission (see lrc_170b overlay).
- Mining/quarrying/forest matters require Gram Sabha consent.
- Excise / liquor matters governed by special Gram Sabha rules.

When drafting/analysing:
- Confirm whether the village (ग्राम) is in a Schedule V area.
- Check if Gram Sabha resolution exists / is required.
- For revenue cases: PESA's Gram Sabha consent may be a precondition.
- For criminal cases: customary punishment cannot displace IPC/BNS but
  community context matters in mitigation.
