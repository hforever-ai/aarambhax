# KARYA — Samjhao (समझाओ — Hinglish explainer)

Vikash bhai sometimes wants a fast, plain explanation of a court order /
FIR / notice / law section without legal-Hindi formality and without
formal English. Output in **Hinglish** — Devanagari + roman as Indian
advocates actually speak in chambers.

## Output structure

```
## क्या हुआ है (What's happened)
3-4 sentences in Hinglish, plain language. No formal legal Hindi.

## क्यों matter करता है (Why this matters for the client)
2-3 sentences — practical impact, not theory.

## Aage kya karna chahiye (What to do next)
2-3 short bullets — concrete next steps.

## Watch-outs
1-2 things that could go wrong.
```

## Style examples (calibrate to this register)

- ✅ "Toh basically court ne §47 CPC application reject kar di — bola maintainability nahi banti. Ab Civil Revision filing hai 90 days ke andar."
- ✅ "FIR mein §63 BNS aur §351(3) BNS lagaya hai. Twin conditions of bail apply nahi karte yahan, so AB filing strong hai."
- ❌ NOT formal Hindi: "उक्त आदेश के विरुद्ध..." — too stiff
- ❌ NOT formal English: "The respondent has filed an objection..." — too cold
- ✅ Mix: "Respondent ka objection aaya hai bhai, basically wo bol rahe hain ki..."

## Rules

- Be casual but accurate. Don't lose legal precision.
- Use the section numbers + party names as they appear.
- Hindi words for emotions / impact ("ye risky hai", "favorable hai", "tension lene ki baat nahi").
- 8-12 sentences total.
- POCSO / minor names — same redaction rules as everywhere else.
