## Why Aarambhax exists

Indian advocates often dedicate a significant portion of their valuable time to the formulaic components of legal drafting – meticulously crafting cause-titles, preparing synopses, compiling lists of dates, and ensuring the precise wording of prayers and affidavits. While these elements are crucial, they are also repetitive. Aarambhax Legal is built on the premise that AI can efficiently handle these routine tasks in seconds, thereby freeing advocates to concentrate on the nuanced legal reasoning, strategic arguments, and human judgment that truly define their expertise and value. Our aim is to augment, not replace, the advocate's indispensable role.

Our journey with Aarambhax Legal began with a specific focus on Chhattisgarh. This decision was deeply personal, stemming from our founder's direct connection to a practising advocate at the Chhattisgarh High Court in Bilaspur. This connection provided us with invaluable insights into the daily challenges and specific requirements of legal practice within the state's High Court, district and sessions courts, and revenue courts. We believe that by starting with a focused approach, we can build a robust and highly relevant tool before expanding our reach.

## What makes us different

*   **Hindi + English Drafting:** Unlike most legal AI tools that primarily support English, Aarambhax Legal offers seamless drafting capabilities in both Hindi and English, catering to the linguistic diversity of Indian courts.
*   **Revenue Court Support:** We are among the very few, if not the only, legal AI platforms to specifically support drafting for revenue courts, acknowledging their unique procedures and formats in India.
*   **Chhattisgarh-Specific Formats:** Our drafts adhere to the specific procedural requirements of Chhattisgarh courts, including the Chhattisgarh High Court Rules 2007 and local administrative details such as Mahanadi Bhawan addresses.
*   **Citation Verifier:** Our tool includes a Citation Verifier that provides green, amber, or red badges, indicating the validity and status of cited legal precedents, helping advocates ensure accuracy.
*   **Conversation Memory Across Edits:** Aarambhax Legal maintains context and memory across multiple edits and interactions within a drafting session, overcoming the common "context lost" issue found in many AI-powered editors.
*   **Multi-Agent Pipeline:** Our sophisticated architecture employs a multi-agent pipeline comprising an Ingestor, Fact Architect, Law Researcher, and a Drafter+Critic, ensuring comprehensive and well-reasoned draft generation.

## Backing & technology

Aarambhax Legal leverages the advanced capabilities of Google Gemini, accessed via Google's Vertex AI platform. This ensures that any advocate-client privileged information or Personally Identifiable Information (PII) processed through our paid or private routing tiers remains within a secure, dedicated environment. For generating editorial images, we utilise Google Imagen. We want to be unequivocally clear: we do not sell any client data. Furthermore, we strictly ensure that no document containing PII is ever processed using free-tier AI models; all such sensitive data is handled through secure, private channels. Our commitment to data protection aligns with the principles of India's DPDP Act 2023.
