## 2. Data We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our service to you. We only collect data that is necessary for the stated purposes.

### 2.1. Account Information

When you register for an account or use our service, we collect the following information:

*   **Personal Identifiers**: Your full name, email address.
*   **Authentication Data**: Your password, which is stored securely using industry-standard hashing techniques (bcrypt). We never store your password in plain text.
*   **Professional Details (Optional)**: Your Bar Council enrolment number, phone number, chamber address, and signature blocks. Providing these details is optional but may enhance the functionality of your drafted documents.

### 2.2. Case Data

Aarambhax Legal is designed to help you draft legal documents. To do this, you will input or upload case-related information. We treat all such data with the utmost confidentiality. This includes:

*   **Case Identifiers**: Case titles, court names (e.g., Chhattisgarh High Court, District & Sessions Courts, Revenue Courts), FIR numbers, relevant legal sections (e.g., BNS, BNSS, BSA, IPC, CrPC).
*   **Party Details**: Names of parties involved (e.g., petitioner, respondent, accused, complainant).
*   **Dates**: Dates of incidents, hearings, filings, etc.
*   **Key Facts**: Factual narratives and details pertaining to the case.
*   **Uploaded Documents**: Any documents you upload for drafting purposes, such as PDFs, judgments, FIR copies, khasra/khata records, and photographs. These documents may contain sensitive personal information and advocate-client privileged information.

### 2.3. Usage Analytics

We collect anonymised usage data to understand how our service is used and to improve it. This data helps us identify popular features, potential areas for improvement, and system performance issues.

*   **Feature Usage**: Counts of which routes or features are accessed (e.g., "draft criminal complaint," "use citation verifier").
*   **Draft Counts**: The number of drafts generated.
*   **Error Logs**: Technical error reports that help us diagnose and fix bugs.

**Crucially, our usage analytics never include the content of your drafts, case data, or any personally identifiable information.** We only track aggregated, non-content-based metrics.

### 2.4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential session cookies to ensure the proper functioning of our website and application. These cookies are necessary for you to log in, maintain your session, and use core features.

*   **Session Cookies**: These are temporary and are erased when you close your browser. They enable us to identify you as a logged-in user and maintain your session state.

**We do not use any third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that track your activity across other websites.** Our use of cookies is strictly limited to what is essential for providing our service.

## 3. How We Use This Data

We use the collected data for specific, legitimate purposes that are essential for providing and improving Aarambhax Legal.

### 3.1. To Generate Legal Drafts via AI Agents

The primary purpose of collecting your case data is to power our multi-agent AI pipeline (Ingestor → Fact Architect → Law Researcher → Drafter+Critic) to generate legal drafts. This involves:

*   **Processing Input**: Analysing the facts, legal sections, and documents you provide.
*   **AI-Powered Drafting**: Using our AI models to construct legal documents based on your inputs and relevant legal frameworks, including the new criminal codes (BNS / BNSS / BSA from 1 July 2024) and older codes (IPC/CrPC) for relevant cases.
*   **Citation Verification**: Utilising the AI to verify citations within your drafts.
*   **Conversation-Memory Editor**: Maintaining context within your drafting sessions to provide a seamless editing experience.

### 3.2. To Send Hearing Reminders via Telegram (Opt-in)

If you explicitly opt-in to our Telegram bot service, we will use your case data (specifically, hearing dates and case titles) to send you automated hearing reminders.

*   **Opt-in Required**: This feature is entirely optional and requires your explicit consent.
*   **Limited Data Sharing**: Only the necessary information (hearing date, case title) is shared with the Telegram bot for notification purposes. No sensitive case facts or documents are transmitted via Telegram.

### 3.3. To Improve Our Prompts and Pipeline (Anonymised Aggregates Only)

We are continuously working to enhance the accuracy and utility of Aarambhax Legal. We use anonymised and aggregated usage data to improve our AI prompts and internal processing pipeline.

*   **No Content Review**: We do NOT review the content of your individual drafts or case data for improvement purposes.
*   **Aggregated Data**: Improvements are based on statistical analysis of how features are used, common drafting patterns (without revealing specific content), and overall system performance.
*   **No Personal Identification**: This process ensures that no individual user or case can be identified.

### 3.4. Our Commitments Regarding Data Usage

*   **No Data Selling**: We do NOT sell your personal data or case data to any third parties.
*   **No Advertising**: We do NOT share your data with advertisers for targeted advertising purposes.
*   **No Training of General Models**: We do NOT use your prompts, inputs, or case data to train Google's general-purpose AI models. Our use of Google Vertex AI is on a paid project, which includes a commitment from Google that your data is not used for training their general models.

## 4. Where Data is Processed

Understanding where your data is stored and processed is crucial for legal professionals. We are transparent about our data infrastructure.

### 4.1. Data Storage

*   **Database**: All your account information, case data, and drafts are stored in our secure database.
*   **Location**: Our database resides on a private Virtual Private Server (VPS) hosted within India.
*   **Provider**: Our VPS provider is Hostinger, with servers located in India.

### 4.2. AI Processing

*   **AI Provider**: Our AI processing, which involves generating drafts and verifying citations, is routed through Google Vertex AI.
*   **Paid Project**: We utilise Google Vertex AI on a paid project. This is a critical distinction, as Google's terms for paid Vertex AI projects explicitly state that your prompts and inputs are NOT used to train Google's general AI models.
*   **No Free Tier Usage for PII**: We strictly ensure that no personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive case data is ever sent through Google's AI Studio free tier, which may have different data usage policies, potentially allowing prompts to be used for model training.

### 4.3. Telegram Bot

*   **Purpose**: The Telegram bot is used solely for sending hearing-reminder notifications, if you opt-in.
*   **Limited Data**: Only the necessary information (hearing date, case title) is transmitted to Telegram for the purpose of sending the reminder.
*   **No Case Data Shared**: No sensitive case facts, documents, or other privileged information is shared with or processed by Telegram.

## 6. Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide you with the service, fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law.

*   **Drafts and Case Data**: Your generated drafts and the associated case data are retained in your account until you actively delete the specific case or request us to delete it. You have full control over the deletion of your case data at any time, in line with your right to erasure under DPDP Act §9.
*   **Account Data**: Your account information (name, email, professional details) is retained for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will retain your account data for an additional 90 days to allow for potential reactivation or to comply with legal obligations before permanent deletion.
*   **Backups**: Encrypted backups of our database are created regularly for disaster recovery purposes. These backups are rotated every 90 days, meaning older backups are securely overwritten or destroyed.
*   **Data Export**: You have the right to request a full export of your data held by Aarambhax Legal at any time. Please contact `privacy@aarambhax.net` for such requests.

## 7. Your Rights under DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have several rights concerning your personal data. Aarambhax Legal is committed to upholding these rights.

*   **Right to Access Your Data**: You have the right to obtain confirmation from us as to whether your personal data is being processed, and if so, to access that personal data and receive information about its processing.
*   **Right to Correction**: You have the right to request the correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, or to complete incomplete personal data. You can typically update your account information directly within the application.
*   **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)**: You have the right to request the erasure of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or if you withdraw consent (where consent is the basis for processing), and there is no other legal ground for processing. This includes the ability to delete your case data and account.
*   **Right to Grievance Redressal**: You have the right to register a grievance with us regarding any act or omission by us in contravention of the provisions of the DPDP Act.
*   **Right to Nominate**: You have the right to nominate any other individual who shall, in the event of your death or incapacity, exercise your rights under the DPDP Act on your behalf.

### How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please send an email to our Data Protection Officer at `privacy@aarambhax.net`. We will respond to your request within 30 days, as mandated by the DPDP Act, 2023. We may require you to verify your identity before processing your request to ensure the security of your data.

## 9. Security

We implement robust technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data and sensitive case information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

*   **HTTPS Everywhere**: All data transmission between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) protocols (HTTPS).
*   **Database Encryption**: Our database backups are encrypted at rest to prevent unauthorised access to your data even in the event of a breach.
*   **Password Hashing**: User passwords are never stored in plain text. They are securely hashed using bcrypt, making them extremely difficult to reverse engineer.
*   **VPS Hardening**: Our Virtual Private Server (VPS) is regularly hardened with security best practices, including firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and regular security updates.
*   **Access Logs**: We maintain detailed access logs for all system administrators and technical support personnel who may access our systems, ensuring accountability.
*   **Breach Notification**: In the unfortunate event of a personal data breach, we commit to notifying affected users and the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act, 2023.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
