## Where we are

We are based in India, building technology for India's legal professionals. Our initial focus is on supporting advocates practising in the Chhattisgarh High Court (Bilaspur), district and sessions courts, and revenue courts, with plans for broader expansion. Aarambhax Legal is a product of AI Aarambh, our parent company, which also operates Shrutam.ai, providing free K-10 education across India. Both products share the vision of giving Indians a fair beginning.
