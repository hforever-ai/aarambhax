# AI Aarambh: Empowering Beginnings Across India

At AI Aarambh, we believe that advanced technology, especially Artificial Intelligence, holds the potential to unlock new opportunities and foster growth for every Indian. Our vision is to build a family of AI-powered tools designed specifically for the unique needs and diverse languages of India. We are committed to creating solutions that are accessible, reliable, and contribute meaningfully to the nation's progress.

Our journey began with a fundamental belief: that access to quality resources should not be a privilege, but a right. This conviction drives us to leverage AI not just for innovation, but for equitable empowerment, ensuring that the benefits of technology reach individuals across various sectors, from education to professional practice.

## Shrutam.ai — What We Already Do

Our first product, Shrutam.ai, launched with the mission to democratise basic education for children across India. It offers free, high-quality educational content for grades 1-10, meticulously covering all major Indian boards including CBSE, ICSE, and various state boards. Shrutam.ai is designed to be truly inclusive, supporting learning in all major Indian languages such as Hindi, English, Marathi, Telugu, and even Hinglish.

Shrutam.ai distinguishes itself through its voice-first, character-driven learning experience. Children interact with "Saavi didi," a friendly and knowledgeable AI persona, making learning engaging and intuitive, especially for those in remote areas or with limited access to traditional schooling. Our unwavering mission with Shrutam.ai is to ensure every Indian child receives quality education, in their own language, completely free of cost.

## Aarambhax Legal — What We're Building Now

Building on the foundation of Shrutam.ai, we are now extending our commitment to empowerment into the legal domain with Aarambhax Legal. We recognise that Indian advocates dedicate countless hours to the meticulous process of legal drafting, time that AI can significantly accelerate without ever replacing their invaluable judgment and expertise. Aarambhax Legal is an AI-powered drafting tool designed to support advocates in their daily practice.

Our initial focus for Aarambhax Legal is on the Chhattisgarh High Court (Bilaspur), along with district & sessions courts and revenue courts within the state. We provide comprehensive support for the new criminal codes (BNS / BNSS / BSA) effective from 1 July 2024, while also retaining support for IPC/CrPC for older cases. Key features include seamless Hindi and English drafting, a robust citation verifier, and a conversation-memory editor that understands context. Our multi-agent pipeline (Ingestor → Fact Architect → Law Researcher → Drafter+Critic) ensures thorough and precise output. Aarambhax Legal is currently in its beta phase and is offered free of charge during this period.

## What Unites Them

The thread connecting Shrutam.ai and Aarambhax Legal is the very meaning of our parent vision: "Aarambh" — a beginning. Both products exist to provide Indians with a fair and strong start. Shrutam.ai offers children a beginning into knowledge and quality education, irrespective of their background or language. Aarambhax Legal offers advocates a beginning into faster, more confident, and more efficient professional-grade drafting.

Both initiatives are built in India, for Indians, with a deep understanding of local contexts, languages, and legal frameworks. Our commitment remains to keep these tools either free or accessible at a very low cost, ensuring that the power of AI is not exclusive but broadly available to those who can benefit most from it.

## What We Will Not Do

*   **Not a Law Firm:** Aarambhax Legal is a drafting tool. We do not provide legal advice, legal services, or engage in the practice of law. Our platform is designed to assist advocates, not to replace their professional judgment or role.
*   **Not a Substitute for Legal Advice:** AI-generated drafts are aids, not final documents. They **always** require thorough review, verification, and customisation by a qualified advocate before filing or use. Advocates remain solely responsible for the content and accuracy of any document filed using our tools, in line with Bar Council of India ethics rules.
*   **Not a Black Box:** We are committed to transparency. Our multi-agent pipeline is designed to be auditable, allowing advocates to understand the steps taken by the AI. We openly disclose that we utilise Google Gemini via Vertex AI for our underlying AI capabilities.
*   **Not Careless with Privacy:** We understand the critical sensitivity of legal data. We handle advocate-client privileged information and Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as FIR numbers, accused names, addresses, and judgments with the utmost care. We are fully compliant with the DPDP Act 2023. Clear distinctions regarding data retention, deletion, consent, and privacy policies between free and paid tiers will be explicitly communicated to our users. Your data security and confidentiality are paramount.
